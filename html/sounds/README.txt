SOUND FILES NEEDED FOR VEHICLE MENU
====================================

Place .ogg audio files in this directory:

1. indicator_click.ogg
   - Short clicking sound for left/right turn indicators
   - Duration: ~0.3 seconds
   - Volume: Low (30%)

2. warning_beep.ogg
   - Beep sound for warning lights and hazard
   - Duration: ~0.5 seconds
   - Volume: Medium (60%)

3. button_click.ogg
   - UI button click sound
   - Duration: ~0.2 seconds
   - Volume: Low (40%)

4. door_open.ogg
   - Door/hood/trunk opening sound
   - Duration: ~0.5 seconds
   - Volume: Medium (50%)

5. door_close.ogg
   - Door/hood/trunk closing sound
   - Duration: ~0.5 seconds
   - Volume: Medium (50%)

6. engine_start.ogg
   - Engine start sound
   - Duration: ~1 second
   - Volume: High (70%)

7. engine_stop.ogg
   - Engine stop/shutdown sound
   - Duration: ~0.8 seconds
   - Volume: Medium-High (60%)

8. window_open.ogg
   - Window rolling down sound
   - Duration: ~0.4 seconds
   - Volume: Low (40%)

9. window_close.ogg
   - Window rolling up sound
   - Duration: ~0.4 seconds
   - Volume: Low (40%)

NOTE: If sound files are missing, the system will gracefully skip playback.
All sounds are controlled by the sounds.js system and can be customized.
